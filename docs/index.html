MusicD Share Card for Android

Turns whatever is playing into a card worth sending — cover, title, artist, year and a few lines about the record. Share it, save it, or post it straight to Discord.

Install

Three steps, once.

  1. Download and open the APK. Allow your browser to install unknown apps if Android asks — you can turn that back off afterwards.
  2. Open the app on the same Wi-Fi as your music. It finds the players itself; there is no address to type in.
  3. Optional — open it on your other devices. The app shows an address like http://192.168.0.42:8747. Open that in a browser on an iPhone, iPad or laptop and you get the same card. On iOS, Share → Add to Home Screen gives it an icon and opens it without browser chrome.

Features

🎵Whatever is playing

Roon, Sonos, or any UPnP / DLNA renderer on the network. It asks whichever one actually knows what the record is.

🖼️A card worth sending

Cover, title, artist and release year, with a few lines about the record and a Pitchfork score where there is one.

📤Share it anywhere

The Android share sheet, press and hold on an iPhone, or a plain download on a desktop.

💬Straight to Discord

Paste a webhook URL once and a button appears on the card. Tap it and the card is posted — under your own name and picture.

🔗Where to hear it

A row of links for the record: the Pitchfork review, and Qobuz, TIDAL, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Deezer and Bandcamp.

🌐On your other devices

The same card opens in a browser on anything else on your Wi-Fi. Nothing to install on the iPad.

⬆️Updates itself

It notices a newer version, downloads it and asks Android to install it. No reinstalling by hand.

🔕Never polls

It asks a speaker what is playing when you open the page or press refresh, and at no other time.
